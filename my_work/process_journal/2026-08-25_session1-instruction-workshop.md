# Session 1 工作坊：同一指令的两种有效解读
Session 1 workshop: two valid readings of one instruction

来源 / source：`Process journal entry #1_wangqiaoli_0825`（学生原件为四页演示稿；此处按作业要求改写入模板，并回答指定的八问。）

## What I set out to do

我想生成一个密集、自由而隐约带有动物意象的线场
My intended outcome was a dense, free field of loops with faint animal associations

**初始指令 / VERSION ONE**

各种大小的圆线，不规则、交叉、不重叠、不平行；使用三种颜色；像动物的意象；自由随机地铺满画面；保持中高密度。
Draw irregular circular lines of different sizes. Let them intersect without coinciding or running parallel. Use three colours. Suggest animal-like imagery. Fill the page freely and randomly at medium-high density.

## 1 · What did you expect to get back? Attach the slip

我预期“圆线”构成抽象主体，“动物”只作为偶然浮现的视觉联想，而不是被直接描绘的对象。
Circular lines should remain the abstract structure; animals should emerge only as incidental associations, not literal subjects.

折叠保存，作为两轮比较的基线

## 3 · Where exactly did the two diverge? Name the words responsible

版本一里两处措辞：

- “像动物的意象”
- “自由随机地铺满画面”

问题：动物可以只是联想，也可以成为被直接描绘的主体；画面填充方式也缺少明确对象。
Problem: the animal could remain an association or become the literal subject; “fill” did not clearly specify the canvas.

## 7 · What did you change in version two, and what did you predict it would do?

**版本二 / VERSION TWO**

- “画面中能隐约看到像动物的造型”
- “圆线画满画布”

修订意图：把动物从“主体”降为“隐约可见的次级线索”，同时明确圆线需要覆盖画布。
Intent: demote the animal from subject to faint secondary cue, while specifying that circular lines should fill the canvas.

第二版只收紧我在意的偏差，并保留随机性
Version two narrows the unwanted divergence while preserving chance
